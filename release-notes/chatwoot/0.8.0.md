# chatwoot 0.8.0

## Changes

### Odoo module chatwoot_bridge ships with the chart

The Odoo side of the bridge is the module `chatwoot_bridge`, in `odoo/chatwoot_bridge` of the
chart. It holds the link fields `x_chatwoot_url`, `x_jira_key` and `x_chatwoot_message_id`,
the four automation rules that send events, the outbox `chatwoot.bridge.event` and its
delivery cron. It names no team, alias or company. The install adopts rules and a cron that
already exist under the same names, so a database that had them in another module keeps
their ids and their active state.

The module reads its settings from system parameter `chatwoot_bridge.url` and from
`CHATWOOT_BRIDGE_SECRET_FILE` or `CHATWOOT_BRIDGE_SECRET`, and signs with
`X-Chatwoot-Bridge-*` headers. The bridge accepts these and the older `X-Grncloud-*` headers.

A record created with context `chatwoot_bridge_no_event` sends no creation event.

### Support conversations move to sales

With `odoo.sales.fromSupport`, the sales label added by staff on a conversation of
`odoo.ticketInbox` turns its ticket into a lead: a `crm.lead` with the transcript in team
`odoo.sales.team`, the ticket cancelled, a new conversation in `odoo.leadInbox` for the
lead, and the old conversation resolved with a note that points to the new one. The label
no longer needs `odoo.website.enabled`; the prepare Job creates it whenever one of the two
uses it. Website chat leads also get `odoo.sales.team`.

### Odoo panel

With `odoo.panel.enabled`, a tab in the conversation sidebar shows the customer's partner,
sale orders, subscriptions, open and closed tickets, leads and opportunities, and the
issues named in `x_jira_key` with their Jira status (`odoo.panel.issues.url`,
`secrets.issueTrackerToken`). With `copilotPanel.enabled` it also shows a summary of three
lines by the agent (Agent panel action `brief`). The panel runs in the `chatwoot-gate` pod
behind the staff login and reads Odoo only. It finds the customer through the linked ticket
or lead, else the contact's email address, else its phone number.

## Upgrade

- New values default to off; a release that sets none of them behaves as before. An
  existing sales label keeps its description.
- To move from an Odoo module that held the bridge before, install `chatwoot_bridge` and let
  that module hand the records over in a migration (remove its xmlids of the adopted rules,
  actions, cron and link fields before its upgrade removes them). Existing links stay.
- `odoo.panel.enabled` needs `sso.enabled`, and the bridge's Odoo user needs read access to
  Sales. `odoo.panel.issues.url` needs `secrets.issueTrackerToken` (vault item
  `chatwoot-issue-tracker-token` with `secrets.type=externalSecret`).
- The prepare Job, the bridge and the gate pod restart once.
