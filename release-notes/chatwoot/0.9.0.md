# chatwoot 0.9.0

## Changes

### Message signature

With `signature.text`, the prepare Job sets that Markdown text as the message signature of
every staff user without one, `{name}` replaced by the staff member's name, and switches it
on in the reply editor for the channel types of `signature.channels`. A signature a user
wrote, and a user who switched it off, stay as they are.

### Formatted staff replies in Odoo mail

The Odoo bridge renders bold, italic, `https` and `mailto` links, `https` images and Markdown
line breaks of a staff reply into the mail Odoo sends. Before, the customer saw the Markdown
marks as text. Anything else stays escaped text.

## Upgrade

- `signature.text` defaults to empty: no signature is set.
- The prepare Job and the Odoo bridge restart once.
