# chatwoot 0.7.1

## Changes

### The prepare Job keeps the sales label

With `odoo.website.enabled` the prepare Job creates the label `odoo.website.salesLabel`, in
lower case, in the account when it is missing, so staff can pick it in a conversation. A
label that already exists keeps its colour and description. An empty
`odoo.website.salesLabel` creates none.

The chart accepts for `odoo.website.salesLabel` ASCII letters, digits, `-` and `_`, at most
64 characters, or an empty value. Chatwoot itself also allows other letters; the chart
refuses them.

## Upgrade

A `odoo.website.salesLabel` with a letter outside ASCII, such as an accented letter, must
be renamed before the upgrade, or the chart does not render. Other values need no change.
The prepare Job and every pod that reads the scripts ConfigMap restart once.
