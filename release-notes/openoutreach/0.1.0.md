### Add the openoutreach chart

The OpenOutreach scout 0.1.0 (ghcr.io/hoolia/openoutreach-scout, pinned by digest) in one
suspended, read-only pod under restricted-v2: human pacing with hard bounds in the values
schema, a kept volume for the browser login and the pacing ledger, a playbook ConfigMap,
signals to ClickHouse or a local file, credentials from an existing Secret, a Secret from
values or a secret store, and an opt-in send path for human-approved messages.
